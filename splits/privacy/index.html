Privacy Policy

Last updated August 7, 2026

Your workout data

Splits does not require an account. Activities, plans, notes, profile information, and training history are stored locally on your device. Splits does not upload this workout data to a Splits server.

Apple Health and Calendar

If you grant permission, Splits can read workout and health information from Apple Health, save workouts to Apple Health, and read or create Calendar events. These integrations are optional. Their data is used on your device to provide the features you request and is not sent to Splits or used for advertising.

Purchases

Splits uses Apple’s StoreKit and RevenueCat to offer and restore Splits Pro. RevenueCat may process purchase and subscription information, app and device information, and an anonymous app-user identifier so Splits can determine whether Pro features are unlocked. This information is used to provide purchase functionality and is not used by Splits for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

Tracking and advertising

Splits does not display third-party advertising, track you across apps or websites, or sell personal information.

Deleting data

You can delete activities within Splits or remove the app to delete its local data. Apple Health and Calendar data can be managed in their respective Apple apps and system settings.
