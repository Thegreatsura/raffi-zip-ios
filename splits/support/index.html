Purchases

If Splits Pro is not appearing after a purchase, open the Pro screen in Splits and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases and refunds are managed by Apple.

Health and Calendar access

Health and Calendar integrations are optional. You can review or change their permissions in the iPhone Settings app under Apps → Splits.

Your data

Splits does not require an account. Your workout plans, notes, and history are stored locally on your device.
